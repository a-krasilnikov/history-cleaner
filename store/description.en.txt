Define the sites you don't want in your browser history. They're deleted the moment you visit them — automatically, forever. Everything else stays untouched, so your history keeps working normally.

No mode to remember to turn on. No cleanup ritual. You add a site once and stop thinking about it.

WHAT YOU GET

• Whole domains or exact sections — example.com, or just example.com/section
• Subdomains included — one rule covers news.example.com, shop.example.com, all of it
• Keep the homepage, drop the rest — optional, per site
• Add the site you're on with a right-click — no typing, no copying URLs
• Clean up existing history in one click, not just future visits
• Your list syncs across every Chrome you're signed into

PRIVACY

It makes zero network requests. There's no server, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no account. It never reads page content — the only address it ever sees is the one you hand it yourself by right-clicking a page. Free and open source — read every line yourself or just build it

PERMISSIONS

History — to delete the entries matching your rules.
Storage — to save your list.
Alarms — to run a periodic background cleanup.
Context menus — to put "Auto-clean history for this site" in the right-click menu.
Active tab — to read the address of the page you right-clicked, only at that moment. There is no standing access to any website. Nothing else.
